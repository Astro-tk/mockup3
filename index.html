Thandiwe is a community development organisation creating opportunities for children, young people and vulnerable communities through education, talent development, youth empowerment, health and social support.

Middelburg, Eastern Cape
One community, many pathways

There is talent in every community. What it needs is opportunity.

Thandiwe brings together education, talent development, youth empowerment, health awareness and community support. These are not separate charity projects. They connect into one journey that walks alongside a young person from their first discovery to a thriving future.

We believe every young person has potential, and that development is not a single intervention but a long relationship with a community.

Our Programmes

Five connected areas of work

Rather than a long list of projects, our work is organised into five pillars that build on one another.

01 Learn

Education

Academic and STEM support that helps learners perform, pass and progress.

02 Discover

Talent & Culture

Ballroom dancing, chess, music and the arts that reveal and grow talent.

03 Grow

Youth Development

Life and career skills that ready young people for work and adulthood.

04 Thrive

Community Wellbeing

Food security, health awareness and support for vulnerable families.

05 Lead

Opportunity

Youth leadership and connections to opportunities that change lives.

Flagship Programme

Thandiwe Ballroom Dancing Programme

Our flagship programme introduces ballroom dancing to learners across our partner schools. It gives every young person a stage, and along the way it builds the habits that carry into the classroom and beyond.

  • Confidence
  • Discipline
  • Creativity
  • Teamwork
  • Self expression

The programme builds towards the Annual Thandiwe Ballroom Dancing Competition, a signature community event launching in 2026.

The Thandiwe Pathway

One journey, five stages

A learner may start with dance or chess, build confidence, move into academic and career support, and go on to become a productive member of the community. Every stage connects to the next.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Ballroom, chess, music and the arts.

  2. 2

    Learn

    STEM, matric support and academic help.

  3. 3

    Develop

    Confidence, life skills and mentorship.

  4. 4

    Prepare

    Job readiness and career guidance.

  5. 5

    Thrive

    Employment, further study and leadership.

A child discovers confidence through dance. Another discovers strategy through chess. These are not separate programmes. They are different pathways to a better future.
